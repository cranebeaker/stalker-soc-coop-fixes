New in v1.5.1

- @xrcore_4gb: IMPORTANT, for everybody on v1.5.0: the engine patch of v1.5.0 could close the game while a level loads (the game crashed at "New game"/level load with the big coop.addon, reading some files inside the add-on failed). Fixed. Run install.bat of this version (or install_xrcore_patch.bat): it replaces the old engine patch by itself, the original file is taken from xrCore.dll.orig. Everybody who plays or hosts needs the new patch
- v1.5.0 by mistake contained the fixes of v1.4.3 only. This version contains everything up to v1.4.7 again: Petrenko's talk window stays open, the Hunter's rifle is in the container, X18 poltergeists (5 electric, 1 fire, no grey screen), Sidorovich's message after Dark Valley, no server crash after talking to Sakharov about the measurements and his bunker door open, NPCs notice players from normal range, the side-job bandits spread out, the Army Warehouses wall explodes at the Duty assault, Semenov on Yantar
- @no_mp_items: Artifacts: price and effects are the original Shadow of Chernobyl ones again. The add-on had raised or changed the price of 25 of the 31 artifacts and made the healing, radiation, bleeding and stamina effects 10 to 40 times stronger than in the original; all of that is back to the original numbers (the immunities already were)
- @cutscenes_pkg_cats: Agroprom: the soldiers in the yard could stay immortal for good. They are invulnerable from Mole's call until the flyover cut-scene ends, and when that scene did not run (Mole was already talked to, or nobody stood in the scene zone) they never became vulnerable. Now they become vulnerable after Mole's dialog, or two minutes after the call at the latest
- Saves of v1.5.0 and earlier keep working
